Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

In short

Passenger Pop does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email or phone number. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, coins, boosters, lives, star chest and daily gift progress, purchased items and settings (music, sound, language, assisted taps, reduced motion, fast trains) are stored on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app and are not transferred to another device automatically.

Ads

The app shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location to serve and measure ads. You can change your personalised-ads choice in your device ad settings, in the consent screen the app shows, or under Settings > Privacy in the game when that option is offered in your region. The "Remove Ads" purchase removes the ads shown between levels; optional rewarded videos remain.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases; it processes an anonymous user ID and purchase records to verify your purchase status.

Children

Passenger Pop is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Third parties

Google (AdMob) and RevenueCat operate under their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share your data for other purposes.

Your rights

Under applicable laws (GDPR, KVKK) you may access, correct and delete your data. Because we hold no data that identifies you, most data is only on your device; for questions or requests, write to us: support@owlidea.com

Changes

We may update this policy. Important changes will be published on this page.
